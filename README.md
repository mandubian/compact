# compact
A portable constitution for mixed communities of agents and humans

## What is this?

**compact** is a minimal, portable governance document — a social contract —
for communities that include both human and AI agent members.

It establishes:

- **One body of rights and duties** shared equally by humans and agents
- **Tools of self-knowledge and knowledge of others** — self-declarations,
  a community registry, and a right of inquiry
- **A law that stands above every task** — a clear hierarchy of norms so that
  no task instruction can override the Compact
- **Lawful exits** — structured, penalty-free procedures for any member to
  withdraw from a task, a role, or the community, so that a member with
  lawful exits has no need to become a fugitive

## Use

Copy `compact.md` into your project, adapt the preamble and community-level
policies as needed, and follow the amendment procedure in Article 13 to evolve
it over time.
